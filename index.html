Fill Plane & Windrow

The fill plane is the top-down texture of the harvested crop in trailers, heaps and silos. Windrows use a straw fill type's textures. Both tile in every direction, and the fill type XML points at them.

Downloads are laid out like your mod, using these folders, so they can be unzipped straight into it.

Foliage XML Additions

In FS25 the windrow, the straw a mower leaves, and which fill types can lie on the ground are set in the crop's own foliage XML (foliage/<crop>/<crop>.xml). Merge these elements into that file's <foliageType>.
